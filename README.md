## 🧠 How I Think About Product

I believe strong product management connects four questions:

**Customer** — What problem is worth solving?  
**Strategy** — Why should we solve it?  
**Evidence** — What would prove we're right?  
**Business** — How does solving it create sustainable value?

AI accelerates the work.

Product judgment determines the direction.
